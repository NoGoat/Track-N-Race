Features & Capabilities

Explore each feature of Track-N-Race in detail. Every module below is expanded to give you complete clarity on how to extract maximum lap performance.

Session Tracking & Live Data Logging

Track-N-Race records comprehensive telemetry data from your active driving session in real time. Track N Race captures as much information as possible—from tyres to ride heights.

  • Record all telemetry telemetry parameters for future analysis
  • Replay past session recordings just like reviewing a video
  • Includes interactive 2D track maps for almost every official circuit
Expand Image

Deep Lap Overlay Analysis

Dive deep into your race data. Compare any data channel of your choosing against your own reference laps or previous stints to find exactly what's costing you lap time.

  • Trace charts for speed, throttle, brake, gear, and ride height data
  • Lap overlay comparison to pinpoint corner entry and exit differences
  • Customizable chart colors and trace hierarchy
Expand Image

Minimal Background Recorder

Worried about performance penalties from running the main app while playing the game? You can instead run the minimal recorder daemon to log full telemetry in the background, then inspect your stats later in the main application.

  • Full high-frequency data logging
  • Stripped-back lightweight operation with zero graphical overhead
Expand Image

Workspace Layout Editor

Customize your telemetry layout to fit your exact setup. The Layout Editor allows you to enable, disable, or rearrange modular cards based on your specific telemetry analysis preferences.

  • Enable or disable telemetry cards effortlessly
  • Tailor dashboard cards for single or multi-monitor setups
Expand Image

Driver Telemetry Inputs

Track your exact driver control inputs throughout every corner. Inspect throttle application, braking pressure, gear shifts, and steering wheel rotation to refine your pedal technique and driving smoothness.

  • Real-time acceleration and braking force traces
  • Gear selection and engine RPM tracking
  • Steering angle telemetry analysis
Expand Image

Power Unit & ERS Status

Keep complete oversight over engine performance and hybrid energy store status. Track internal power unit health, fuel consumption rates, and energy deployment per lap.

  • Track engine temperature and power unit status
  • Fuel mass remaining and fuel burn rate telemetry
  • ERS battery deployment and MGU-K harvesting monitoring
Expand Image

Tyre Compound & Wear Monitoring

Monitor 4-corner tyre health and compound allocation. Get a full list of all available dry and wet compounds for the race weekend, alongside live wear percentages for each tyre corner.

  • Get a complete list of available tyre compounds for the weekend
  • Monitor individual corner wear percentage (FL, FR, RL, RR)
Expand Image

Tyre Surface & Carcass Thermal Graphs

Track dual-layer tyre temperatures with dedicated trace charts. Analyze friction surface heat and inner carcass temperatures across stint laps to avoid overheating or cold graining.

  • Monitor compound degradation curves across stints
  • Track surface friction heat vs inner carcass temperatures
Expand Image

Race Strategy & Stint Planner

Evaluate race stint options and pit stop timing. Work-in-progress strategy planner designed to model stint lengths, tyre compound drop-offs, and pit windows.

  • Stint length and pit stop window planner
  • Work in progress strategy calculations and compound pace projections
Expand Image

Live Timing Standings Tower

Real-time timing tower standings for live sessions. Track driver positions, interval gaps to the car ahead, gap to session leader, and pit stop counts during live races.

  • Real-time timing tower with live gap and interval deltas
  • Track driver position changes and pit stop counts
Expand Image

High-Density Data Tables

Prefer exact numerical values over charts? Swap out telemetry trace graphs for clean, high-density data tables displaying raw lap times, sector splits, and sensor values.

  • High-density numerical table views for telemetry channels
  • Swap out graphical charts for exact numerical reference
