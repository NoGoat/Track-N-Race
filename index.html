Track N Race is a telemetry recording and analysis tool designed for the F1 games. Analyze and find out where you lose that extra tenth over your competitors. Supports F1 24, F1 25 and the 2026 DLC pack.

Track almost Everything

Track N Race tries to track as much information as possible from the apps. Everything from tyres to ride heights can be tracked.

  • Record everything for future analysis
  • Replay recordings like a video
  • Includes track maps for almost* every circuit
    • *I do not own F1 24. So I couldn't get maps for any tracks exclusive to F1 24.

Deep Analysis

Dive deep into your race data. Compare any data of your choosing against other of your own laps to find what's costing you lap time.

  • Trace charts for almost any data
  • Lap overlay comparisons
  • Customizable colors and chart hierarchy
Expand Image

Minimal Recorder

Worried about performance penalties from running the app while playing the game? You can instead run the minimal recording and view your stats later in the main app.

  • Full data logging
  • No additional features to reduce overhead
